A PTT matrix on an ESP32-S3 with Ethernet.

- 8 PTT inputs, each mappable to any of 8 keying outputs for amplifiers and radios
- Mapping is configured from a web page served by the board
- Runs from the station's 13.8 V supply
- Outputs only ever short to ground
